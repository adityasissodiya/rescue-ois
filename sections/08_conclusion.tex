\section{Conclusion}

Incident command needs one attributable record of the commander's decisions, written at
the scene by the vehicle that the command hierarchy designates. \systemname{} separates
what that record needs: authority from the command hierarchy, enforced by core-issued
epochs, a holder check, and a lease; sequencing at the command edge; and replication by
outboxes and forwarding, where any eventually consistent mechanism, a CRDT included,
would do. Without an out-of-band channel, a designated writer cannot escape the
trade-off of \S~\ref{sec:tradeoff}, so \systemname{} states its position: recording
continues for one lease window, command moves as soon as the new edge reaches the core,
and the core's journal stays single-writer while two edges may overlap for up to $L$.
Raft instead moves authority to the reachable quorum, and a last-writer-wins CRDT lets
the merge decide; R3 rules out both.

Future work is lease-wait promotion, admitting late entries by epoch, binding authority
to the commander's credential, non-blocking delivery of decisions to responders, adding
the lease to the TLA+ model, and deployment on a vehicle-mounted mesh.
