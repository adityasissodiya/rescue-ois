\section{Conclusion}

This paper presents an authority-aware, offline-first incident-information architecture for rescue-service operations under intermittent connectivity. The central design move is a tiered synchronization model that preserves one command-side writer for authority-bearing incident-journal state while allowing disconnected responders to continue durable forward submission. The prototype and model checking support the feasibility of this trade-off in Docker Compose emulation and in the bounded abstract promotion model.

The design deliberately does not provide automatic multi-writer failover. Strict promotion and fencing are required to avoid split brain, and the current implementation validates that rule at model level rather than as a crash-hardened production mechanism. Field-radio evaluation, full crash-injection validation, Android tablet evaluation, WireGuard/mTLS measurement, and production hardening remain future work.
