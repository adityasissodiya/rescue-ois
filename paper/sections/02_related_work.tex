\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

Three bodies of work bear on the question: how human-in-the-loop systems transfer
authority, how distributed systems keep one holder of a role and decide when an
authorization is final, and how incident command works. \Cref{tab:positioning} summarizes
where each sits.

\subsection{Authority Transfer in Human-in-the-Loop Systems}
\label{sec:related-hitl}

\textit{Shared control and arbitration.} Robotics and vehicle research mostly replaces a
binary handover with continuous arbitration, shifting authority between a human and
automation by weights that follow intent, confidence, workload or
delay~\cite{abbink2012haptic,abbink2018topology,darvish2023teleoperation}. Flemisch et
al.\ separate authority, ability, responsibility and control, and argue that the four must
stay in balance for a transfer to be safe~\cite{flemisch2012balance}. We use the same
separation: a person's authority to command, which the organization confers, and a
device's control over what enters the journal, which the system exercises on that person's
behalf.

\textit{Transfer as a protocol.} Handover and takeover are usually modelled as multi-step
protocols with alerting, acknowledgement and confirmation that the receiving actor has
entered the control loop~\cite{gil2019designing,gil2020engineering,saito2018transfer},
or negotiated so that two actors never seize control at once~\cite{rothfuss2022negotiation}.
Human-factors work is consistent that takeover quality depends on the receiver's situation
awareness, and that transfers fail when the human is out of the
loop~\cite{endsley2017autonomy,grindley2024handover}. Our appointments that take effect on
acceptance (\cref{sec:acceptance}) are the distributed counterpart of this confirmation
step, for the case where the confirmation itself is delayed by a partition.

\textit{Lost links.} When the link fails, the confirmation loop breaks, and systems revert
to behaviour authorized in advance: lost-link procedures for remotely piloted aircraft,
minimum-risk manoeuvres for remote driving~\cite{reyesmunoz2023rpas,sakakeeny2024lost}.
Even this fallback is not universally safe; braking on connection loss can itself cause
collisions~\cite{brettin2025blindspot}. A link indicator can also stay nominal while the
operator no longer has trustworthy control~\cite{sonaglio2026connectivity}. Pre-authorized
deputy activation (S4, \cref{sec:policies}) is a lost-link procedure for command authority,
and the commander's own timeout is its fallback; both inherit these problems, and we show
that a device stopping its commander is the command analogue of an emergency stop that is
not always safe (\cref{sec:eval-costs}).

\textit{What is missing.} We found work on transfer between human operators under degraded
links mostly at the level of interfaces and architectures~\cite{adekoya2026multioperator,rebolo2025c3},
and none on how authority, stale permissions and the decisions made meanwhile are resolved
once links return. \pending{Back this with a documented systematic search; the current
basis is an AI-assisted literature review (Consensus, October 2026).} Those two gaps are
where this paper sits. The setting also differs in kind: authority here passes between
people, not between a person and automation; it concerns decisions taken over minutes, not
control inputs at millisecond rates; and the record of who held authority when is itself
an artifact that commanders, crews and reviewers rely on.

\subsection{One Holder at a Time: Leaders, Leases and Fencing}
\label{sec:related-leases}

Leases bound how long a holder may act without contact~\cite{gray1989leases}, lock services
grant them from a replicated coordinator~\cite{burrows2006chubby,hunt2010zookeeper}, and
fencing tokens let a resource refuse a holder whose lease has lapsed~\cite{kleppmann2017ddia}.
Lease protocols have been machine-checked, including their timing
arithmetic~\cite{trencseni2026paxoslease,sharma2023grove}. Consensus and replicated state
machines order the commands of successive leaders by view or
epoch~\cite{oki1988viewstamped,junqueira2011zab,ongaro2014raft}, and Vertical Paxos lets an
external master reconfigure the replicas~\cite{lamport2009vertical}, the role the duty
chief plays here. In all of these, either a quorum chooses the holder, or the coordinator
that grants the lease must be reachable. Neither holds at an incident: the organization
names the commander, and the coordinator---the regional server---is the first thing a
partition cuts off. Heartbeat-bound credentials come closest to our enforcement: a
credential is valid only while its parent's heartbeats are fresh, which bounds how long a
revoked agent can act by a window set by the heartbeat schedule and the clock
skew~\cite{deochake2026heartbeat}. There, revocation means the parent stops beating. In
succession by silence the successor keeps beating, and its relayed heartbeats are what can
revive the old holder (\cref{sec:ack}).

\subsection{Authorization and Finality under Weak Consistency}
\label{sec:related-finality}

Signed delegation lets a device check authority offline~\cite{lampson1992authentication,rfc2693spki,sudarsan2021survey},
and freshness bounds make revocation effective offline within a known
window~\cite{stubblebine1995recent}. Weakly consistent replicas can keep data eventually
consistent while access-control policy is temporarily inconsistent~\cite{wobber2010policy}.
Jacob and Hartenstein separate an operation's causal authorization, decided from the
history its issuer knew and final once made, from its topological authorization in the
eventual total order, which a concurrent revocation can still
overturn~\cite{jacob2025eventually}. Our provisional and residual orders apply that
distinction to command; we do not claim the distinction. What we add is when the second,
revisable verdict becomes final without consensus between devices, for authority that one
person holds at a time (\cref{prop:settlement}). Confidentiality, integrity and
accessibility cannot all hold in a highly available
system~\cite{yanakieva2021impossibility}, and blockchain protocols resolve the
availability--finality dilemma by an available ledger with a finalized
prefix~\cite{neu2021ebbandflow}, which our provisional and authoritative statuses resemble;
there, finality comes from a quorum, here from the completeness of the records of the
people who could still change the verdict. Bayou distinguished tentative from committed
writes in weakly connected storage~\cite{terry1995bayou}. Invariant confluence and the CALM
theorem identify what can run without coordination~\cite{bailis2014coordination,hellerstein2020calm};
judging a decision by the grant it was made under is not monotone, which is why settlement
needs a completeness condition. Finally, a final verdict does not end an authorization's
effects: earlier authorized work can still cause an effect the application
rejects~\cite{santosgrueiro2026effect}. At an incident the effect frontier is a crew acting
on an order, beyond any system's control, so we cannot claim closure; we measure how many
orders crews saw as valid before they turned residual, and design to reduce it.

\subsection{Incident Command and Emergency Networks}
\label{sec:related-ics}

Incident command systems combine hierarchical and network forms, coordinating many
organizations under a temporary hierarchy~\cite{moynihan2008combining,moynihan2009network}.
We model only the designated part: one commander at a time, changed by explicit or
pre-agreed acts. Military command and control faces the same disconnected, intermittent and
limited links, and tactical middleware tolerates them~\cite{suri2009tactical}, but does not
decide who may issue an order. Public-safety and edge research places resilient transport,
storage and consensus near responders~\cite{kumbhar2015legacy,wang2023overview,sagor2024distressnetng,jahanian2022conice};
CoNICE reaches consensus among infrastructure-less peers, an assumption of no privileged
peer that incident command removes. Multi-writer replicated data
types~\cite{almeida2014delta,hanssen2025emergency} and local-first
software~\cite{kleppmann2019localfirst} serve the replication of field data well, but as
an authority mechanism they let every replica decide. \pending{Still to cover: how Swedish
rescue services transfer command in practice, once the rescue service has been asked; and
