\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

A device that obeys an isolated holder cannot know whether the holder is loyal. In our
definitions, any isolation reach is also a hostile tenure in some run. Seed-v0 keeps the
hostile tenure below $H + 2\eps$ after withdrawals take effect, and it keeps commands of
different holders apart by $g - 2\eps$, if the candidate arguments hold. Executable
scenarios, an independent checker, negative controls and a small bounded model support
these statements but do not prove them. The same evidence shows that a Byzantine
composition of known pieces behaves the same in our runs. The next step is therefore a
decision about the contribution (\cref{sec:memo}), informed by experiments E1 to E3, and
not further polishing of the mechanism.
