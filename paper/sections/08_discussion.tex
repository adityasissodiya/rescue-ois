\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{Is There a Contribution Beyond Known Compositions?}
\label{sec:memo}

URSA already removes authority by quorum renewal and expiry, with no message to the
device~\cite{luo2004ursa}. The draft of 11 November 2026 claimed that no removal rule had
both properties. That claim is false, and we have withdrawn it \claim{C-G4}. Exclusive succession is
the grantor rule of quorum leases~\cite{vanrenesse2011nerio}. Our adaptation of that rule
to signatures, device checks and Byzantine quorums (\texttt{xl}) matches seed-v0 in every
directed comparison, and it avoids one of seed-v0's stalls. A candidate argument for
\texttt{xl}'s separation exists in the positioning record. Like the arguments for seed-v0,
nobody has reviewed it.

We therefore do not claim a new mechanism. We see four options and do not choose among
them here.
\begin{enumerate}
  \item A \emph{characterization} paper: the problem, the definitions and the bounds, with
  the limit credited to the indistinguishability tradition. It would include a reviewed
  proof that the composition \texttt{xl} meets the bounds, and seed-v0 would be one
  instance. The risk is that reviewers see a straightforward composition.
  \item Find a \emph{real difference}: a property that epochs give and \texttt{xl} lacks,
  such as an order of terms across holders, audit, or re-appointment of the same holder.
  This option closes only with a checked counterexample or proof.
  \item \emph{Change the question}, for example to changes of the officer set during
  partitions, or to limits on the damage that an authorized hostile holder can do. Each
  needs its own problem statement and prior-art search.
  \item An \emph{evaluation} paper that compares published designs under one
  hostile-holder model with this scenario-and-checker method. This needs faithful
  comparators and review.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Threats to Validity}

\emph{Unreviewed arguments.} Every argument is a candidate, and the specification, code
and paper were drafted with an AI assistant. \emph{Shared authorship.} The checker shares
no code with the kernel, but it shares an author. \emph{Directed runs.} Each scenario
fixes one schedule, and the comparison values depend on the schedules that we chose.
\emph{Model gaps.} The TLA+ model has exact clocks, one verifier and tiny bounds, and the
simulator uses constant clock offsets. \emph{Prior art.} We read five sources in full. Two
recent sources and the forward citations of URSA and Nerio are unread. \emph{Domain.}
The grid example motivates the problem. No operator was consulted, and the parameters do
not come from operational data.

\subsection{Open Questions and Next Experiments}
\label{sec:open}

The roadmap in the artifact ranks the open questions. The first three experiments, each
with its criterion for closing, are as follows.
\begin{enumerate}
  \item[E1] \emph{Composition decision.} Model \texttt{xl} in TLA+ at the bounds of
  \cref{sec:tla}, run every seed-v0 scenario against an \texttt{xl} translation, and write
  its proof. \emph{Closes when} a person has reviewed a proof that \texttt{xl} meets
  \cref{prp:order,prp:wte,prp:avail}, or a checked counterexample shows a property that
  seed-v0 has and \texttt{xl} lacks.
  \item[E2] \emph{Prior-art closure.} Read HRCred (in particular what it does under partition) and
  the work by Stantchev in full, follow the forward citations of URSA and Nerio, and search
  for Byzantine and signed leases and for IEC~62351-8 role-based access. \emph{Closes when}
  the positioning record has page-pinned entries for each, and any overlap with the
  definitions or bounds is recorded.
  \item[E3] \emph{Review and wider checking.} Have a person review \cref{app:arguments}, and
  run the bounded model with $\eps > 0$, two verifiers, epoch~3 and withheld Byzantine
  signatures. \emph{Closes when} each claim's status in the ledger changes on review, and
  the wider configurations finish with their expected outcomes.
\end{enumerate}
Questions for the domain remain open. Which operations must continue when a control room
is isolated, and for how long? Where would a verifier sit, and what clock holdover gives
$\eps$? Who may authorize the removal of an operator? These need people who operate grids.
